## Topics Covered

### Molecular Biology

**Molecular Biology** is the study of how molecules inside living cells work together to run life.

Main molecules:

| Molecule | Function |
|----------|----------|
| DNA | Stores genetic information |
| RNA | Carries genetic instructions |
| Protein | Performs body functions |
| Enzymes | Speed up chemical reactions |

---

## DNA Replication (Advanced)

DNA replication is the process of making an exact copy of DNA before cell division.

### Replication Fork

When DNA opens during replication, it forms a **Y-shaped structure** called the **Replication Fork**.

- DNA opens into two strands.
- This is the starting point for DNA copying.

---

## Leading vs Lagging Strand

| Leading Strand | Lagging Strand |
|----------------|----------------|
| Continuous DNA copying | DNA copied in small pieces |
| No gaps | Small fragments are formed |
| Faster process | Fragments are joined later |

### Easy Memory

- **Leading = Continuous**
- **Lagging = Pieces**

---

## Okazaki Fragments

**Okazaki Fragments** are short DNA pieces formed on the **Lagging Strand** during DNA replication.

- They are produced because the lagging strand cannot be copied continuously.
- These fragments are joined together later.

---

## DNA Ligase

**DNA Ligase** is a glue-like enzyme that joins Okazaki fragments into one continuous DNA strand.

| Component | Role |
|-----------|------|
| Okazaki Fragments | Small DNA pieces |
| DNA Ligase | Joins the fragments |

---

## DNA Replication Flow

DNA Opens → Replication Fork → Leading Strand (Continuous) + Lagging Strand (Pieces) → Okazaki Fragments → DNA Ligase Joins Them

---

## Key Takeaways

- DNA replication begins at the replication fork.
- The leading strand is copied continuously.
- The lagging strand forms Okazaki fragments.
- DNA Ligase joins the fragments to complete the new DNA strand.
