# Day 25 – Misfolded Proteins and Diseases

## What are Misfolded Proteins?

Misfolded proteins are proteins that fail to fold into their correct three-dimensional structure. They may lose their normal function or accumulate in cells, contributing to disease.

## 1. Alzheimer's Disease
- Associated with the accumulation of amyloid-beta plaques and abnormal tau protein tangles in the brain.
- These changes are associated with neuronal damage and cognitive decline.

## 2. Parkinson's Disease
- Associated with the abnormal aggregation of alpha-synuclein protein.
- These aggregates are linked to the loss of dopamine-producing neurons.

## Key Takeaway

Correct protein folding is essential for normal cellular function. Abnormal protein folding and aggregation are associated with several neurodegenerative diseases.
