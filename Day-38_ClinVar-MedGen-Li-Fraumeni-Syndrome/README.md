# Day 38 – ClinVar and MedGen: Li-Fraumeni Syndrome

## Objective
To explore NCBI clinical genetics resources and understand disease information associated with the TP53 gene.

## Database
- NCBI ClinVar
- NCBI MedGen

## Disease Information

- **Disease Name:** Li-Fraumeni Syndrome
- **Abbreviation:** LFS
- **MedGen UID:** 88399
- **Concept ID:** C0085390
- **Related Gene:** TP53
- **Chromosome Location:** 17p13.1
- **Mode of Inheritance:** Autosomal dominant

## About Li-Fraumeni Syndrome

Li-Fraumeni syndrome is an inherited cancer predisposition syndrome associated with an increased risk of developing different types of cancer.

It is commonly associated with changes in the TP53 tumor suppressor gene.
