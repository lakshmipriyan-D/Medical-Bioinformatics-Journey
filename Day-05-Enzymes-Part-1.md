# Day 05 – Enzymes (Part 1)

Today I learned the fundamentals of enzymes and how they interact with substrates to speed up chemical reactions.

## Topics Covered

### Enzyme
- A special protein that speeds up chemical reactions in the body.
- Enzymes are not destroyed after a reaction and can be reused.

### Substrate
- The molecule on which an enzyme works.
- Each enzyme has a specific substrate.

### Active Site
- The specific region of an enzyme where the substrate binds.
- Chemical reactions take place at the active site.

### Lock & Key Model

| Lock & Key | Biology |
|------------|---------|
| Lock | Enzyme |
| Key | Substrate |
| Keyhole | Active Site |

- Only the correct substrate fits into the enzyme's active site.
- After the reaction, the product is released and the enzyme can be reused.

## Examples

| Enzyme | Substrate |
|---------|-----------|
| Amylase | Starch |
| Lactase | Lactose |
| Catalase | Hydrogen Peroxide |

## Learning Flow

Enzyme → Active Site → Substrate → Reaction → Product → Enzyme Reused
