# Day-08 – Molecular Biology (Transcription, Translation & Codons)

## Overview

Today I learned how genetic information flows from DNA to proteins through **Transcription** and **Translation**. I also learned how **codons** work, including the **start codon (AUG)** and **stop codons (UAA, UAG, UGA).**

---

# 1. Transcription

**Definition:**

Transcription is the process of copying genetic information from **DNA** to **mRNA**.

## How it works

1. DNA inside the nucleus opens.
2. RNA Polymerase copies the DNA sequence.
3. mRNA is formed.
4. mRNA leaves the nucleus and travels to the ribosome.

### Important Point

- DNA contains **A, T, G, C**
- mRNA contains **A, U, G, C**
- **Uracil (U)** replaces **Thymine (T)** in RNA.

### Example

DNA:

```text
TATTCG
```

mRNA:

```text
AUAAGC
```

---

# 2. Translation

**Definition:**

Translation is the process where the **ribosome reads mRNA** and builds a protein.

## Steps

1. mRNA reaches the ribosome.
2. The ribosome reads **3 letters at a time**.
3. Each group of 3 letters is called a **codon**.
4. Amino acids join together to form a protein.

---

# 3. Codons

A **codon** is a group of **three RNA letters** that tells the ribosome which amino acid to use.

### Example

```text
AUG GCU UAA
```

- AUG → Start
- GCU → Amino Acid
- UAA → Stop

---

# 4. Start Codon

- **AUG**
- Starts protein production.
- Codes for **Methionine**.

### Memory Tip

> AUG = Start = Methionine

---

# 5. Stop Codons

These codons tell the ribosome to stop building the protein.

| Stop Codon | Function |
|------------|----------|
| UAA | Stop |
| UAG | Stop |
| UGA | Stop |

---

# DNA → Protein Flow

```text
DNA
 ↓

mRNA
 ↓
Ribosome
 ↓
Translation
 ↓
Protein
```
