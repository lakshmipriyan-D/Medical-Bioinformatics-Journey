# Day 06 – Enzymes (Part 2)

Today I learned how temperature affects enzyme activity, how enzyme inhibitors work, and the functions of common enzymes in the human body.

## Topics Covered

### Temperature Effect

| Temperature | Enzyme Activity |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Low Temperature | Works slowly |
| Optimum Temperature (37°C) | Works at maximum speed |
| High Temperature | Denaturation – enzyme stops working |

**Denaturation:** High temperature changes the enzyme's 3D shape, so the substrate cannot fit into the active site.

---

### Enzyme Inhibitors

An enzyme inhibitor is a molecule that reduces or stops enzyme activity.

| Type | How it Works |
|------|--------------|
| Competitive Inhibitor | Blocks the active site, so the substrate cannot bind. |
| Non-Competitive Inhibitor | Binds to another part of the enzyme and changes its 3D shape. |

---

### Common Enzymes

#### Amylase

- **Location:** Mouth (Saliva)
- **Substrate:** Starch
- **Function:** Breaks starch into simpler sugars.

#### Lactase

- **Location:** Small Intestine
- **Substrate:** Lactose (Milk Sugar)
- **Function:** Breaks lactose into smaller sugars for digestion.

**Lactose Intolerance:**

- Less lactase enzyme.
- Milk cannot be digested properly.
- Causes gas, bloating, and diarrhea.

#### Catalase

- **Location:** Body Cells
- **Substrate:** Hydrogen Peroxide (H₂O₂)
- **Function:** Breaks harmful hydrogen peroxide into water and oxygen.

**Reaction:**

H₂O₂ → H₂O + O₂
