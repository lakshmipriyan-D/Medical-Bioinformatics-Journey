# Day 14 – Human Genome Project (HGP)

## What is the Human Genome Project?

The Human Genome Project (HGP) was a large international scientific project carried out from **1990 to 2003**.

Its main goal was to determine the DNA sequence of the human genome and create a useful reference for genomic research.

---

## Main Goal of HGP

The primary goal was to:

- Determine the sequence of the human genome.
- Identify human genes.
- Create genetic and physical maps of the genome.
- Store and make genomic information available for research.
- Develop better technologies and computational tools for genome analysis.
- Study human genetic variation.
- Address ethical, legal and social issues related to genomic information.

---

## Uses of the Human Genome Project

The information generated by HGP helps researchers to:

- Study human genes and their functions.
- Understand the genetic basis of diseases.
- Identify genes associated with disease.
- Study genetic variation between individuals.
- Support biomedical and genomic research.
- Develop better approaches for disease diagnosis and treatment research.
- Develop databases and computational tools for storing and analysing genomic data.

---

## Key Takeaway

The Human Genome Project provided a foundational reference for modern genomics by mapping and sequencing human DNA and creating resources that researchers can use to study genes, genetic variation and human disease.
