# Day 23 – Protein Folding

## What is Protein Folding?

Protein folding is the process by which a newly synthesized protein folds into its specific **3D structure**.

The correct 3D structure is important for the protein to perform its biological function.

---

## Chaperone Proteins

Chaperone proteins are helper proteins that assist newly synthesized or misfolded proteins in achieving their correct 3D structure.

They help prevent incorrect protein aggregation.

---

## Chaperone Workflow

Protein synthesis
↓
Unfolded protein
↓
Chaperone binds
↓
Folding assistance
↓
Correctly folded protein
↓
Functional protein

---

## Key Takeaway

Protein folding determines the functional 3D structure of a protein, while chaperone proteins help proteins fold correctly and prevent harmful aggregation.
