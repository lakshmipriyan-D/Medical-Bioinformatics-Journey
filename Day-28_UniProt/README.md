# Day 28 – UniProt

## What is UniProt?

UniProt (Universal Protein Resource) is a database that provides detailed information about proteins.

## What Information Can We Find in UniProt?

1. **Protein Name** – Name of the protein.
2. **Accession Number** – Unique identifier for a protein entry.
3. **Gene Name** – Gene associated with the protein.
4. **Organism** – Species from which the protein originates.
5. **Reviewed Status** – Shows whether the entry is reviewed (Swiss-Prot) or unreviewed (TrEMBL).
6. **Protein Function** – Biological role of the protein.
7. **Protein Sequence** – Amino acid sequence.
8. **Protein Length** – Number of amino acids.
9. **Isoforms** – Different versions of a protein produced from the same gene.
10. **Protein Domains** – Functional regions that can be explored through linked resources such as InterPro.

## Example: TP53

- Gene Name: TP53
- UniProt Accession: P04637
- Protein Name: Cellular tumor antigen p53
- Organism: Homo sapiens
- Protein Length: 393 amino acids
- Main Function: Helps regulate cell-cycle arrest, DNA repair and apoptosis.

## Importance of UniProt

UniProt helps researchers explore protein sequences, functions, and related biological information.

**Key Takeaway:** UniProt is an important resource for protein analysis in bioinformatics.
