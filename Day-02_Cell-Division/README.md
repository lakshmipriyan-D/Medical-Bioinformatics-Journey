# Day 2 – Cell Division (Mitosis & Meiosis)

## Topics Learned

- Cell Division
- Mitosis
- Meiosis
- Chromosome Distribution
- Polar Bodies

## What is Cell Division?

Cell division is the process by which one cell divides to produce new cells.

## Mitosis

### Purpose

- Growth
- Wound repair

### Process

1. One body cell contains 46 chromosomes.
2. DNA is copied.
3. The cell divides.
4. Two identical cells are produced.

### Result

- 1 cell → 2 cells
- Each cell has 46 chromosomes.

## Meiosis

### Purpose

To produce sperm and egg cells with half the chromosome number.

### Process

1. One cell starts with 46 chromosomes.
2. DNA is copied.
3. Meiosis I produces two cells with 23 chromosomes each.
4. Meiosis II produces four cells with 23 chromosomes each.

### Result

- 1 cell → 4 cells
- Each cell has 23 chromosomes.

## Important Point

- Father contributes **23 chromosomes** through sperm.
- Mother contributes **23 chromosomes** through the egg.
- The baby receives a total of **46 chromosomes**.
- In females, one mature egg is formed, while the remaining three cells are called **polar bodies**.

## Mitosis vs Meiosis

| Feature | Mitosis | Meiosis |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Purpose | Growth & Wound Repair | Sperm & Egg Formation |
| Final Cells | 2 | 4 |
| Chromosomes | 46 → 46 | 46 → 23 |
| Cells Produced | Identical | Different |

## Key Takeaways

- Mitosis maintains the body.
- Meiosis prepares reproductive cells.
- Human babies receive 23 chromosomes from each parent.
