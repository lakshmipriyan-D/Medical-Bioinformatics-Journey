# Day 29 – Protein Sequence in UniProt

## What is a Protein Sequence?

A protein sequence is the ordered arrangement of amino acids that form a protein.

## Protein Sequence in UniProt

UniProt provides protein sequences along with information about their function, length, and accession number.

## Importance of Protein Sequences

1. **Protein Identification:** Helps identify proteins.
2. **Function Prediction:** Helps understand protein functions.
3. **Disease Research:** Supports the study of disease-associated proteins.
4. **Sequence Comparison:** Helps compare proteins across species.
5. **Drug Research:** Supports protein-target research.

## Example: TP53

- **Gene:** TP53
- **UniProt Accession:** P04637
- **Protein Length:** 393 amino acids
- **Protein Name:** Cellular tumor antigen p53

## Key Takeaway

UniProt protein sequences are useful for protein identification, functional analysis, and bioinformatics research.
