# Day 26 – Mass Spectrometry

## What is Mass Spectrometry?

Mass spectrometry (MS) is an analytical technique used to identify and measure molecules based on their mass-to-charge ratio (m/z).

In bioinformatics, it is widely used to identify proteins and study their composition.

## Working Steps

1. **Sample Preparation:** The biological sample is prepared for analysis.
2. **Ionization:** Molecules are converted into charged ions.
3. **Mass Analysis:** Ions are separated based on their mass-to-charge ratio (m/z).
4. **Detection:** The ions are detected and their signals are recorded.
5. **Data Analysis:** The resulting mass spectrum is analysed to identify molecules or proteins.

## Applications

- Protein identification
- Protein characterization
- Biomarker research
- Drug discovery
- Proteomics studies

## Key Takeaway

Mass spectrometry helps researchers identify and characterize biological molecules by measuring their mass-to-charge ratio.
