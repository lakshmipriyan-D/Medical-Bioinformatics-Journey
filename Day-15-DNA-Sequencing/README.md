# Day 15 – DNA Sequencing

## What is DNA Sequencing?

DNA sequencing is the process of determining the exact order of nucleotides (A, T, G, C) in a DNA molecule.

## Main Goal

To identify the nucleotide sequence of DNA.

## Basic Workflow

DNA Sample → Sequencing → DNA Sequence → Analysis

## Applications

- Genetic research
- Disease research
- Genome analysis
- Bioinformatics

## Key Takeaway

DNA sequencing tells us the exact nucleotide order present in a DNA sample.
