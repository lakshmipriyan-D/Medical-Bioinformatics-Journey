# Day 27 – Mass Spectrometry: Real-Life Applications

## 1. Cancer Biomarkers
- Identifies proteins and molecules associated with cancer.
- Helps researchers discover potential biomarkers for early detection.

## 2. Drug Discovery
- Identifies and analyzes chemical compounds.
- Helps study drug composition and interactions with target molecules.

## 3. Disease Diagnosis
- Detects and measures specific molecules in biological samples.
- Helps diagnose certain metabolic disorders and other diseases.

## Key Takeaway
Mass spectrometry measures the mass-to-charge ratio of ions and helps scientists identify and analyze molecules in biological samples.
