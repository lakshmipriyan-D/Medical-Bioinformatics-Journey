# Day 24 – Misfolded Proteins

## What are Misfolded Proteins?

Misfolded proteins are proteins that fail to fold into their correct three-dimensional structure.

A protein's function depends on its correct structure. When a protein misfolds, it may lose its normal function or form harmful aggregates.

## How Does Protein Misfolding Occur?

Protein misfolding can occur due to:

- Genetic mutations
- Changes in cellular conditions
- Errors during protein folding
- Environmental stress

## Protein Folding vs Misfolding

| Normal Folding | Misfolding |
|---|---|
| Correct 3D structure | Incorrect 3D structure |
| Normal function | Loss or change of function |
| Usually functional | May form harmful aggregates |

## Diseases Associated with Misfolding

Misfolded proteins are associated with several diseases, including:

- Alzheimer's disease
- Parkinson's disease
- Huntington's disease
- Prion diseases

## Role of Chaperone Proteins

Molecular chaperones help newly produced proteins fold correctly.

They can also help prevent abnormal protein aggregation and assist some misfolded proteins in refolding or being removed.

## Bioinformatics Relevance

Bioinformatics can help study:

- Protein sequences
- Protein structures
- Mutations affecting protein folding
- Protein structure prediction

Tools such as **AlphaFold** can be used to predict protein structures from amino acid sequences.

## Key Takeaway

Correct protein folding is essential for normal biological function. Misfolding can lead to loss of function, protein aggregation, and disease.
