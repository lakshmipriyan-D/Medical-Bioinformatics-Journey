# Day 37 – UniProt: TP53 Protein Analysis

## Objective
To explore the UniProt database and understand the protein information associated with the human TP53 gene.

## Database
UniProtKB (Universal Protein Resource)

## Protein Details
- **Protein Name:** Cellular tumor antigen p53
- **Gene Name:** TP53
- **UniProt Accession:** P04637
- **Organism:** Homo sapiens (Human)
- **Protein Length:** 393 amino acids
- **Entry Status:** Reviewed (Swiss-Prot)
- **Protein Existence:** Evidence at protein level

## Protein Function
The p53 protein acts as a tumor suppressor and helps regulate:
- Cell cycle arrest
- DNA repair
- Apoptosis (programmed cell death)

It plays an important role in maintaining genomic stability.

## Key Learnings
- Explored the UniProtKB database.
- Identified the protein name and accession number.
- Understood protein length and amino acid sequence.
- Learned the biological functions of p53 protein.
- Explored protein sequence and isoform information.

## Protein Sequence
The canonical TP53 protein sequence contains 393 amino acids.
