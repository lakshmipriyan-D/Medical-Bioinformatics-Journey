# Day 21 – Applications of UniProt

UniProt is a protein database that provides information about protein sequences, functions, and characteristics.

## 1. Disease Diagnosis
Helps researchers understand proteins associated with diseases.

## 2. Cancer Biomarkers
Helps identify and study proteins that may serve as cancer biomarkers.

## 3. Drug Discovery
Provides protein information that supports the identification of potential drug targets.

## 4. Personalized Medicine
Helps researchers study protein variations that may influence individual responses to treatment.

## 5. Infectious Diseases
Provides information about proteins in pathogens such as bacteria and viruses.

## 6. Bioinformatics
Supports protein sequence analysis, functional annotation, and biological research.

## Key Takeaway
UniProt is an important resource for studying proteins and supporting biomedical and bioinformatics research.
