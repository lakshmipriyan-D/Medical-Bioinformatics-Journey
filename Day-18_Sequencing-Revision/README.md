

## Sanger Sequencing

- Also called chain-termination sequencing.
- Uses ddNTPs to stop DNA synthesis.
- Suitable for small, targeted DNA regions.

## Next Generation Sequencing (NGS)

- Modern high-throughput sequencing method.
- Sequences millions of DNA fragments in parallel.
- Produces large amounts of sequence data.

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Sanger | NGS |
|---|---|---|
| Throughput | Low | High |
| Sequencing | Targeted | Massively parallel |
| Data output | Small | Large |
| Best for | Small regions | Large-scale sequencing |

## Key Takeaway

Sanger → Small targeted sequencing

NGS → Large-scale, high-throughput sequencing
